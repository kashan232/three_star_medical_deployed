Three Star Medical ERP - System Overview
    System Architecture & Workflow Overview
    April 2026 | Version 1.0

Core Modules and Architecture
    Commercial: Purchase & Sales Lifecycle
    Logistics: Warehouse, Inventory & Batch Tracking
    Accounts: COA, Vouchers & Ledgers
    Reports: PDF Reporting & Statistics
    HR: Biometry & Payroll

Detailed Purchase Cycle (Inward)
    1. Purchase Order (PO): Procurement Planning
    2. Goods Receipt Note (GRN): Physical Stock Entry
    3. Batch & Expiry: Logic for Lot Tracking
    4. Purchase Return (PRN): Returning defective/excess stock
    5. Vendor Ledger: Impact on Payables

Detailed Sale Cycle (Outward)
    1. Sale Order (SO): Booking and Reservation
    2. Delivery Note (DN): Warehouse Dispatch & Packing
    3. Sale Receipt Note (SRN): Final Invoicing & Billing
    4. Sale Return (SR): Customer Returns & Credit Notes
    5. Customer Ledger: Impact on Receivables

Chart of Accounts (COA)
    Level 1: Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, Expense
    Level 2: Control Accounts
    Level 3: Party Accounts (Vendors & Customers)
    Level 4: Transactional Accounts
    All entries follow Double-Entry Accounting (Dr = Cr)

Voucher Management System
    Receipt Voucher (RV): Records money coming in
    Payment Voucher (PV): Records payments sent out
    Expense Voucher (EV): Records operational costs
    Journal Voucher (JV): Non-cash adjustments
    Vouchers provide the audit trail for all money movements

Inventory & Batch Management
    Batch Tracking: Every unit is linked to a GRN
    Expiry Control: Prevent sales of expired medicine
    FEFO: First Expiry, First Out distribution
    Multi-Warehouse support and Stock Transfers
    Units of Measure (UOM): Handling Boxes vs. Pieces

Reporting and Analytics
    Vendor & Customer Ledger Statements
    Profit and Loss Calculation
    Stock-on-Hand Reports
    Daily Transaction Summaries
    High-fidelity, print-ready documents (GRN, Invoice)
